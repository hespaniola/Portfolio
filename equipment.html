My Gear

Every shot tells a story — and these are the tools I use to capture it. My setup is built for versatility: from cinematic drone footage to handheld storytelling and immersive 360° shots.

DJI Mini 4 Pro

My go-to drone for cinematic aerials. Compact, stable, and perfect for dynamic establishing shots. I use it for everything from chase scenes to landscape B-roll.

drone 4K aerials
Panasonic Lumix G7

My main camera for cinematic work. Shoots crisp 4K with great color depth. I pair it with manual lenses to get that authentic, film-style look.

4K cinematic handheld
Insta360 GO 3S

Tiny but powerful action cam. I use it for quick, creative angles — like mounting on cars, helmets, or handheld rigs for first-person or BTS perspectives.

action stabilized POV
DJI FPV

For dynamic chase shots and fast movement. The FPV adds intensity and adrenaline to reels — perfect for automotive action and wide cinematic fly-ins.

fpv drone action
Insta360 X4

My favorite for immersive 360° content and creative transitions. Lets me reframe shots in post and create impossible perspectives.

360° reframing creative
