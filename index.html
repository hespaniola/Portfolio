02 — Leadership & Values

Leadership Style

My leadership style is goal-oriented, direct, and focused on taking action. I like setting high standards, creating a clear direction, and helping a team stay focused on the outcome we are trying to achieve. At the same time, I have learned that strong leadership also requires patience, self-awareness, and the ability to stay calm when situations become difficult. I want to be the kind of leader who takes responsibility, communicates clearly, and helps the people around me perform at their best.

Personal Values

Some of my most important values are accountability, authenticity, discipline, growth, and fairness. These values influence the way I approach school, business, fitness, and relationships because I want my actions to match the standards I expect from myself.
